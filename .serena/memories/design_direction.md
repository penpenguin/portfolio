# Design Direction

- `DESIGN.md` is the source of truth for visual changes.
- The portfolio now uses a Linear-inspired direction: precision, quiet product surfaces, hairline borders, restrained typography, subtle hover states, compact CTAs, and screenshot-led presentation.
- Preserve the existing BentoUI grid composition, section structure, and content order. Do not reorganize cards, sections, routes, or page hierarchy for polish work.
- Allowed polish scope: card padding, border, surface, typography, hover, CTA, and screenshot presentation.
- Avoid loud gradients, decorative spotlight effects, heavy shadows, and large layout changes.
